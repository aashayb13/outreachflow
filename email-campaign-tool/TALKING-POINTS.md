# Email Campaign Tool — Client Talking Points

## The Problem
- Outbound email platforms (Mailchimp, HubSpot Marketing) charge per contact and lock you into their ecosystem
- No control over sending infrastructure, deliverability, or data
- Generic tools don't integrate cleanly into custom lead-gen pipelines

## What This Is
A **self-hosted email marketing platform** purpose-built for outbound sales automation — campaigns, drip sequences, analytics, and API-driven workflows, all under your control.

## Key Selling Points

### 1. Zero Per-Contact Costs
You own the platform. No monthly fees that scale with your list size. Send through your own Gmail/SMTP — pay nothing beyond hosting.

### 2. Full Automation Ready
Built-in **drip sequences** with configurable delays. Enroll leads automatically via API as they come in from your pipeline (Cornersight, Lead Magic, web forms, etc.).

### 3. Pipeline Integration
REST API and webhooks let this slot directly into your existing stack:
- New lead captured → auto-added to list → enrolled in sequence
- Lead opens email → webhook fires → CRM updated
- Works with n8n, HubSpot, Zapier, or any webhook consumer

### 4. Real-Time Analytics
Track opens, clicks, bounces, and unsubscribes per campaign and per contact. Know exactly what's working without waiting for a third-party dashboard to update.

### 5. Compliance Built In
Unsubscribe handling, bounce tracking, and suppression lists are baked in — not bolted on.

### 6. You Own Your Data
Contacts, templates, analytics — everything lives in your database. Export anytime. No vendor lock-in.

## Who It's For
- Field services companies running outbound to ICP lists
- Agencies managing multi-client email outreach
- Any team that wants predictable costs and full control over their email pipeline

1. Show the **Dashboard** — overview stats at a glance
